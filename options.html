🎯 LinkedIn Job Search Filter - Settings

Configure filters to automatically hide jobs that don't match your criteria on LinkedIn

❌ Exclude Keywords (Jobs to Hide)

Jobs with these words in the title will be hidden
Example: Technical
Engineer
Sales
Client Success
Jobs with these words anywhere will be hidden
Example: medical
fintech
cybersecurity
client-facing
Jobs in these locations will be hidden
Example: Redwood City
Foster City
New York

✅ Include Keywords (Jobs to Highlight)

Jobs must have at least one of these words in the title (leave empty to disable)
Example: Senior
Program Manager
PMO
Highlight jobs in these locations (leave empty to disable)
Example: Remote
Hybrid
Pleasanton

⚙️ Filter Behavior

Turn off to disable all filters temporarily
When unchecked, filtered jobs will be dimmed but still visible
Display the reason when hovering over filtered jobs
Hide jobs that have been reposted (likely filled or stale positions)
Hide jobs posted more than this many days ago (leave empty to disable)
Examples: 7 = Only show jobs from the past week
1 = Only show jobs from the past 24 hours
30 = Only show jobs from the past month

☁️ Dashboard Sync (optional)

Optional - local job saving works exactly the same with or without this. When configured, jobs you save here are also sent to your Job Saver web dashboard. The token is stored only in this browser (never synced, never exported), and can be revoked at any time from the dashboard's Extension Connection section.
